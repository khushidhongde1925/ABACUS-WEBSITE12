Welcome to Smart Brain Abacus Classes!

Smart Brain is guided by Surekha Dhongde, who helps children learn Abacus in a simple, friendly and enjoyable way.

Through regular practice, students can develop their calculation skills, concentration, speed and confidence.

Our goal is to make learning numbers interesting and enjoyable for every child.
